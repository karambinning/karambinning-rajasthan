About the restaurant

The kitchen,
and the people in it.

Rajasthan is a small Indian and Bangladeshi restaurant on Railway Road, in the part of King's Lynn that does the unshowy daily work of feeding people.

We took the name not because the menu is strictly Rajasthani — North Indian and Bangladeshi cooking is what the kitchen does best — but because of what the word carries with it. Long marinades. Patience with spice. Heat that builds rather than ambushes. A table that's been laid for guests and means it.

"Cook the marinade out properly, and the gravy looks after itself."

The room seats a comfortable number — small enough that we notice when someone walks in for the second time, big enough to take a banquet booking on a Friday without losing the run of the night. We're licensed, so a glass of beer with the curry is sorted. We serve dine-in, takeaway, and delivery across the town — call the restaurant for delivery area and minimum order.

The kitchen is steady-handed about hygiene. The most recent rating from King's Lynn and West Norfolk Council is 5 — Very Good, awarded June 2024.

Tuesday is the night off. Every other night, from five until eleven, the tandoor is hot and the phone is on.

Three small things we take seriously.

Time before heat

Marinades are given the hours they ask for. Spices are toasted before they go into the gravy. Shortcuts taste like shortcuts.

The vegetarian half

Vegetarian and vegan dishes are a proper section of the menu, not a polite afterthought. The brinjal bhaji is doing more work than most starters.

The room you sit in

Tables that aren't on top of each other. Lighting that's actually warm. A waiter who knows what's on the special board tonight.
